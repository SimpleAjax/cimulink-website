Sales and Material Planning

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful answers before we discuss your business system.

Is Cimulink only an ERPNext company?

No. ERPNext and Frappe are important implementation platforms, but Cimulink also works across process design, automation, integrations, AI, custom applications, and infrastructure.

Do you start by recommending software?

No. We first understand the process, current tools, information flow, responsibilities, and operational gaps. The right answer may be configuration, integration, automation, or custom software.

Which industry are you focusing on first?

Manufacturing is the first specialized offer, with a dedicated focus on sales, materials, purchasing, production, quality, dispatch, accounts, and management visibility.

Can you connect software we already use?

Yes. Cimulink can connect existing ERP, CRM, websites, forms, email, WhatsApp, accounting tools, APIs, and internal applications where the integration is technically appropriate.
